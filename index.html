Manager & Admin Onboarding Guide

Complete system walkthrough, team PIN distribution, and payday execution guide for Operations Manager.

1

Admin Master Access & Confidential Mode

Log into DevTrack using the Administrator profile with master PIN 9999. This gives Operations Manager exclusive access to view developer hourly rates, run team payroll, approve attendance requests, and manage settings.
🔒 Security Note: Developer staff cannot view each other's hourly rates or earnings. Rate confidentiality is enforced across all public and team views.
2

Configure Employee Profiles, Rates & 4-Digit PINs

Go to ⚙️ Settings → 👥 People & Pay Rates. Click the ✏️ Edit button next to each developer's name. A profile editor modal will open with the following fields to configure:
👤
Full Name & Job Title
Update the real name and assign an official role title (e.g. Lead Full-Stack Developer, Senior QA Engineer, Backend Developer).
💵
Currency & Hourly Rate ($ USD)
Enter the agreed hourly wage in USD. Select the base currency. The system will auto-convert to ₱ PHP on payslips using the live exchange rate you set.
🔑
Private 4-Digit Access PIN
Assign each employee a unique 4-digit PIN (e.g. 1234, 5678). This is their personal clock-in key — never share it with other team members.
🌴
Leave Credits (VL / SL / EL)
Set the number of leave days available for each type: Vacation Leave (VL), Sick Leave (SL), and Emergency Leave (EL). Standard: VL=12, SL=10, EL=5. Adjust per employment contract.
📧
Email Address (Optional)
Add the employee's work or personal email for reference. Appears on payslips and correspondence templates.
💡 Tip: Do this for all team members before sending out login PINs. Once saved, developers can immediately start logging their daily attendance.
3

Send Logins & Onboard the Team

Once profiles and PINs are configured, copy and send this message to each staff member via Slack, Skype, or Email:
Hi team! We are now using DevTrack for daily attendance, time off, and payroll.

🌐 App URL: https://diverse-ideas-attendance.vercel.app
🔑 Your Access PIN: [Your 4-Digit PIN]

Daily Steps:
1. Open the link on your laptop or phone.
2. Tap your profile name and enter your 4-digit PIN.
3. Click Time IN at shift start, Break for lunch, and Time OUT when finishing work.
4. To file Leaves or missed time punches, use 📁 My Stuff → Apply → Certificate of Attendance (COA) / Leave.
4

Daily DTR Monitoring & 1-Click COA Approvals

  • Live Attendance Tab: Check who is currently working, on break, or offline in real-time.
  • Time Off & Holidays Tab: View pending Leave and Certificate of Attendance (COA) applications. Operations Manager can approve or reject requests with 1-click.
  • Work Schedules: Mondays auto-default to Work From Home (WFH) and Tuesdays-Fridays default to Onsite.
5

Payday Payroll & Official 1-Page Payslip Vouchers

On payday, go to 💰 Timesheets & Payday:
  • Live USD → PHP Conversion: Real-time exchange rate ($1 USD = ₱58.50 PHP) automatically computes converted gross earnings for bank / GCash disbursement.
  • Quick Payslip Bar: Select the staff member and click [ Run & Print Payslip ].
  • 1-Page Printable Voucher: An executive dual-currency payslip statement will pop up, formatted strictly for 1-page A4 printing or PDF export with approval signature lines.
6

Managing Projects & Client Codes

Go to ⚙️ Settings → 📁 Projects & Activities. As admin, Operations Manager can:
  • Add New Projects: Create project codes (e.g. LOGI) and names for developers to clock into.
  • Edit Projects: Update project name, description, or status at any time.
  • Archive Completed Projects: Mark finished projects as Completed so they no longer appear in clock-in options.
7

Holidays, Time Off & Leave Approvals

In the 🌴 Time Off & Holidays tab:
  • Add Philippine / CDO Holidays: Use the quick preset selector to add national or CDO local holidays (e.g. Higalaay Festival, Charter Day). Custom company events can also be added.
  • Approve or Reject Leave: Pending leave applications from staff appear here. Operations Manager reviews the reason and approves or declines with 1 click.
  • COA (Certificate of Attendance): If a developer forgot to clock in/out, they file a COA. Operations Manager approves to correct the timesheet record.
8

Database Safety & Local Backups

In ⚙️ Settings → 💾 Database & Backups, Operations Manager can click "Download JSON Backup" at any time to save a local backup copy of all team timesheets, logs, rates, and requests.

Leave & COA Approvals (Time Off Tab)
